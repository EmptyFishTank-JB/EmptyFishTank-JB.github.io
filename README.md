# EmptyFishTank-JB.github.io

EmptyFishTank Studio: home of our games and apps, with their privacy policies and app-ads.txt.
Published by GitHub Pages at https://emptyfishtank-jb.github.io/ (the main branch, as it is).

- `index.html`: the studio's home page, a card for each game or app.
- `app-ads.txt`: the AdMob publisher line, at the site's root where AdMob looks for it
  (https://emptyfishtank-jb.github.io/app-ads.txt). One line covers every app on the AdMob
  account; the site must be the **developer website** in each app's Google Play listing.
- `bytefall/privacy.html`: ByteFall's privacy policy, the URL its Play listing uses:
  https://emptyfishtank-jb.github.io/bytefall/privacy.html
- A new app: a folder of its own (`appname/privacy.html`) and a card on `index.html`.
- `.nojekyll`: the files served as they are (no Jekyll).
